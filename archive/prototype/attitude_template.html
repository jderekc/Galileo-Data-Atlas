Burns and thruster events

Plume length (and cone width) is drawn proportional to duration × force (impulse, N·s), and brightness increases with it, only when both numbers are present in the event list. If either is missing the event gets a hollow marker and the model shows an "unknown" ring at a schematic engine location; nothing is estimated.
