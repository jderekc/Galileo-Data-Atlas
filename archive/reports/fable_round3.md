## Regressions / new defects (most severe first)

R1. **HUD grew from 137 to 172 px in the default state** (48 % of the 1400x900 stage, up from 38 %) because "nearest sample 0 s" wraps onto its own line at the 340 px max-width, the presence line adds a row, and "(table)" lengthens the moon line. Consequence at **1024x700 default**: the legend-aware max-height (214 − 20 − 60 = 134 px) now clips \|B\|, Br, B (JSE) and the presence line out of view with no indicator (r12 shows the cut mid-line). In the round-2 build these were visible at 1024. Fix: drop lines by priority instead of clipping — in `updateA` compute the available height (`stage.clientHeight − 20 − legendH`) and omit, in order, the layer block, the presence line, B (JSE), the moon line, until the card fits; and put "nearest sample" on the same row as the status chip or shorten it to "Δ 0 s" so the orbit line does not wrap. Also consider `max-width:360px` (the 340 px cap is what forces the wrap).
R2. **Silent truncation remains** wherever the card does not fit (Graph focus at 1400; 1024 with layers): no "…" or fade, so a viewer cannot tell that B (JSE) or the layer summary exists. Same fix as R1, or a 12 px bottom gradient plus "▾ more" that expands on click.
R3. **Presence-glyph semantics**: instrument values exist only on the field file's 4-minute grid, so during any MAG gap every instrument shows "○" (r06: "MAG ○ EPD ○ PLS ○ PWS ○ HIC ○ DDS ○") even where the instrument's own file may have samples that this atlas does not carry. The About key ("○ … the segment has values but none at this time") lets a viewer read "EPD ○" as "EPD had no data" — a statement about the archive the atlas cannot make. Reword the key and the HUD tooltip to "○ no value at this 4-minute point of the field file" and "∅ no values for this instrument in this segment's points". Related: DDS "●" is true when the bin is coded 1 ("0 or below range"), and the notes say a DDS zero "can mean no events or no operation" — "has a value" is accurate to the atlas but the glyph key could say "● a value (including a coded zero)".
R4. Status chips look interactive (`cursor:pointer`, hover border) but are not — see defect 4 note.
R5. Phone: HUD 336×166 px (38 % of the 439 px stage), legend 5 rows / 82 px; no overlap (gap 169 px) and no horizontal scroll (scrollWidth 390). No phone regression beyond the HUD height growth in R1.
