## Regressions / new defects (most severe first)

1. **Spacecraft off-screen at the new T = 0 start for 9 segments** (item 4). Line 559 `S.TA=…:0` + `fitA()`; fix in `fitA`/`loadSeg` as above.
2. **"This orbit" twice with no group label between 881 and 1180 px** (item 5a). CSS block at lines 133–139 (`.grp2 .gl{display:none}`). Fix: keep the labels but shorten them ("Run"/"Graph" are already short — hide only the Layout label), or rename the chips when compact ("Play: all / Play: this", "Graph: orbit / Graph: mission") via `data-short` attributes and a `::before` content rule.
3. **Sub-10 px text at ≤ 1180 px** (item 5b). `--u` floor 0.85 applied to already-small 11–11.5 px styles. Fix: apply `--u` only to ≥ 12.5 px styles (body, h1, .sub, chips, kv, notes) and keep `#hud`, `#legend`, `.src`, `.lyr small`, `.gl` at their fixed sizes; or raise the floor to 0.92.
4. **Side-panel toggle appears on phone** (b_phone_top / `#sideTog` at y = 4654, 74×45): pressing it hides the whole side column on a phone (applyLayout sets `#side.hidden` regardless of `mobileQ`); only the *restore* is guarded. Out of scope this round, but it is a new phone control — guard the click as well, or hide the chip under the mobile query.
5. Labels unaware of the HUD and of horizontal edges (item 1 ii–iii): "to the Sun" under the HUD in top views; "Noon · LT 12" clipped at the right. Fix: add the HUD rect (and a 0..W clamp on x0/x1 with a horizontal nudge for anchor-0.5 labels) to the `placed` list in `layoutLabels`.
6. Leader lines cross other labels (item 1 i), and ring numbers form a vertical ladder in dense views. Fix: let ring numbers (pri 4) hide instead of move when displaced > 20 px.
7. Dock 3–7 px below the viewport with 6 strips at 1024x700 and 1280x720 (item 5).
8. "Key" chip 15 px tall; "Side panel" chip last in the tab order (item 5c–d).
9. Hash rewritten every second while paused (item 3) — cosmetic; skip when unchanged.
